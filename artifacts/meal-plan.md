## Overview

2 weeknight dinners for 2, both at or under the 30-minute cap, nothing spicy, and no protein
repeated across the two nights (chicken then beef). Night 1 uses the on-hand chicken breast,
broccoli and rice directly — the perishables scheduled first per pantry guidance. Night 2 was
reassigned from Garlic Butter Shrimp to Ginger Beef Stir Fry: the shrimp recipe's stated 10-minute
total covered only the shrimp-and-sauce component and left the pantry rice/broccoli side
unverified against the 30-minute cap (gate 1 fail on the prior attempt). Ginger Beef Stir Fry's
stated 30-minute total is self-contained — it covers its own protein and vegetable (green beans)
end to end with no untimed side left over — so the cap is verifiably met. The trade-off is that
Tuesday no longer shares the pantry rice/broccoli with Monday and needs three net-new pantry
items (soy sauce, sesame oil, cornstarch) plus fresh green beans; this is the smallest change that
resolves the gate 1 finding without touching Monday. Budget/costing against the $40 cap is scoped
to shopping-list-builder and budget-aggregator, run next from this selection.
